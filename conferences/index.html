Committees, Conferences, Workshops and Summer Schools

I am the Regional Editor for Europe of the International Journal of Performability Engineering. I am a member of the Editorial Board of Transactions on Computational Collective Intelligence and an Associate Editor of the Vietnam Journal of Computer Science.

I am a member of the IEEE Reliability Society Technical Committee on Systems and Software Assurance and of the IEEE SMC Technical Committee on Computational Collective Intelligence.

I am a member of the Board of Directors of the Tarot Summer School on Software Testing. The last edition of the Summer School took place in Naples (Italy).

I am a member of the QRS Steering Committee. The last edition of the conference took place in Hangzhou (China).

I am a member of the ICCCI Senior Programme Committee. The last edition of the conference took place in Ho Chi Minh City (Vietnam).

I am a member of the A-MOST Steering Committee. The last edition of the workshop took place in Naples (Italy), collocated with ICST 2025.

I am a member of the SEFM Steering Committee. The last edition of the conference took place in Toledo (Spain).

I was a member of the ICTSS Steering Committee (formerly known as TestCom/FATES) for the period 2009-2012 and of the DISCOTEC Steering Committee for the period 2019-2024.

During 2026 I am involved in the following events:

  • ICECCS 2026: 30th Int. Conf. on Engineering of Complex Computer Systems (PC Member)
  • ACIIDS 2026: 18th Asian Conference on Intelligent Information and Database Systems (PC Member)
  • ICCCI 2026: 18th Int. Conf. on Computational Collective Intelligence – Technologies and Applications (PC Member)
  • QRS 2026: 26th IEEE Int. Conf. on Software Quality, Reliability & Security (PC Member)
  • ICCIES 2026: 2nd Int. Conf. on Computational Intelligence in Engineering Science (PC Member)
  • SSBSE 2026: 18th Symposyum on Search-Based Software Engineering (PC Member)
  • ICTSS 2026: 38th IFIP Int. Conf. on Testing Software and Systems (PC Member)
  • DSA 2026: 13th Int. Conf. on Dependable Systems and their Applications (PC Co-Chair)
